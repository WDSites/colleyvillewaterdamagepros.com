This site has no contact form. Water damage does not wait for a reply, so we take requests by phone only. Email is for questions about this website, our privacy policy or records of a past call.

What to have ready when you call

  • Where the water came fromA pipe, an appliance, the roof, a storm or a drain. If you do not know, say so.
  • What it touchedRooms, floors, walls, ceilings and any belongings.
  • How longWhen it started and whether it is still moving.
  • Your address and ZIPSo we can confirm service and a crew location.

If it is an emergency right now

Shut off the main water valve if you can reach it safely. Stay out of water that touches outlets or the panel. Call 911 for shocks, sparks or a gas smell. Then call the line. The emergency water removal page has a longer checklist.

Where we are

The office address is where we receive mail. Crews travel to you, so there is no need to visit. For ZIP codes and towns we serve, see the service area page.

Water spreading right now?

Call the dispatch line and describe what you see. We will explain the scope and price before work starts.
