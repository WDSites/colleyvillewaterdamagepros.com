Start with the situation, not the trade name

Water damage work happens in stages, and each service page covers one of them. The first stage is stopping and removing the water. The next is drying the structure and cleaning what the water touched. The last is rebuilding, and mold is handled on its own page because it follows its own standard.

What every job has in common

Every job starts with a conversation. The dispatcher asks where the water came from, what it touched and whether it is still spreading. Before work starts, the scope is explained and so is the way the price is built, and you decide whether to go ahead.

Crews are licensed and insured professionals who work the Colleyville area, and they record readings and photos so you have documentation for your insurer. We never promise that a claim will be approved, and your policy decides what is covered. The Texas Department of Insurance explains the basics at tdi.texas.gov.
