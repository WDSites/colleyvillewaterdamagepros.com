Where Remington Park is

Remington Park is in the northwest part of Colleyville. Community Impact described it as a master planned community of about 180 homes, mostly built out by 2005, with a clubhouse, pool, courts, a park and a neighborhood lake or pond.

That makes it one of the newer areas we cover in Colleyville. County listing records place streets such as Remington Parkway, Horseshoe Bend and Polo Trail in the community. We do not claim official boundaries and the home count is an estimate from the profile.

Newer homes still leak

A newer house is not immune. Supply hoses to washers and dishwashers, ice maker lines, water heaters and irrigation connections fail at any age. The damage usually comes from a part that wore out or was fitted badly, not from the age of the neighborhood.

We have no data on how often water damage occurs in Remington Park. What we can offer is a clear process for the leaks that do happen, starting with how to stop the source and what to record before cleanup.

SourceWhat to do first
Washing machine hoseClose the two wall valves, pull the machine out only if the floor is dry and call
Water heaterShut the cold water inlet and, for a gas unit, call the gas utility if you smell gas
Ice maker or fridge lineClose the small stop valve behind the unit and check cabinets beside it
Irrigation or hose bibClose the shut-off, then look for water along the foundation and in the garage

The valve nearest the source is usually the quickest one to close.

Keep it safe

Water around a water heater or an outlet can be electrically hazardous. Do not step into it.

  • Do not touch a breaker or plug while standing on a wet floor.
  • Skip a household vacuum for anything beyond a small clean spill.
  • Photograph the damage and the failed part if you can do so safely.
  • Contact the utility or emergency services for any immediate danger.
