Where Saddlebrook is

Saddlebrook is a Colleyville subdivision bordered by Cheek Sparger Road on the south and Colleyville Boulevard on the west, according to a 2014 Community Impact profile. The paper estimated about 246 homes built out by 1993, with greenbelts, rolling hills and trails, near Sparger Park.

Storms, roads and what we can and cannot say

Colleyville has closed low water crossings after heavy rain. On May 8, 2019, Community Impact reported four closures, on Bedford Road, Oak Knoll Drive, Cheshire Drive and Jackson Road. In 2020 a resident told the city that Bedford Road across from Sparger Park floods and gets closed.

Those accounts are about roads. They are not reports of water in Saddlebrook homes, and we do not say that this neighborhood floods. They do show why a storm can delay travel around the park, so call early and expect detours.

If rain gets into the house

Rain intrusion usually comes through a roof, a window, a door threshold or a wall. Find where the water entered, take photos and move belongings away from the wet spot. Stopping the entry point is a roofer's or builder's job, while ours is the water already inside.

Stay safe while it is wet

Never wade through water that may be near outlets, cords or a panel.

  • Do not touch a ceiling that is sagging or dripping near a light fixture.
  • Keep children and pets out of rooms with standing water.
  • Use a flashlight, not a switch, if the lights are on a wet circuit.
  • Call the utility or emergency services for gas smells, sparks or any immediate danger.

Drying after storm water

Storm water can leave drywall, insulation and flooring wet well past the visible edge. Crews extract the water, then dry the structure and recheck readings. Storm water damage walks through the stages, and flood damage restoration covers larger intrusions.

Water that came from the ground or a drain may be contaminated. Ordinary fans and a shop vacuum are not enough for that kind of water, and some soaked materials must be removed.

Before you call your insurer

Take dated photos and keep damaged items until your insurer has seen them. Whether a rain related loss is covered depends on the cause and the policy, and flood and storm losses are often treated differently. We cannot promise coverage.
